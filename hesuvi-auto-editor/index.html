    About the calibration

    Use an unedited stereo capture of the supplied eight-event 7.1 Dirac stimulus, including LFE. One timing shift applies to every direction and both ears. The supplied calibration places the median pair peak at sample 53; this is an operational timing convention. Identical impulses cannot verify speaker routing, or distinguish capture-start delay from renderer latency.

    The output contains seven stereo pairs in HeSuVi channel order. LFE is checked and excluded. Processing preserves relative levels and ear timing. The local report includes the recording filename and SHA-256 hash. Files stay in your browser.
